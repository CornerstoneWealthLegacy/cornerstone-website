Fees & Pricing

What does a Florida attorney cost?

No one likes surprise legal bills. Here's exactly how Truestead Law charges — flat fees where we can, contingency where it's fair, and a free consultation and written quote before you commit to anything.

Estate Planning — flat fee

Florida Estate Kit from $399 (self-guided) · $950 (attorney-guided)

Wills, trusts, powers of attorney, and healthcare directives at a known, flat price. The online Florida Estate Kit covers most families; custom or higher-net-worth plans are quoted as a flat fee after your free consultation. See what estate planning costs in Florida →

Personal Injury — contingency fee

No fee unless we recover

You pay no attorney's fee up front and none at all unless we obtain a recovery for you. The fee is a percentage of the recovery, disclosed in writing before you sign. Clients may be responsible for costs. Personal injury →

Real Estate, Asset Protection & International — flat or hourly, quoted up front

Agreed in writing before we begin

Deeds, titling, contract review, entity and trust structuring, and cross-border work are quoted up front — flat fee where the scope is clear, hourly where it isn't. You approve the number before any work starts. Real estate · Asset protection · International

Probate & Trust Administration — statutory / quoted

Guided by F.S. § 733.6171

Florida sets a statutory fee framework for formal probate that scales with the estate. Summary administration costs far less. We tell you which applies and quote it early. See Florida probate costs & how to avoid them →

Elder Law & Medicaid Planning — flat fee

Quoted after a free consultation

Medicaid planning, asset protection, and incapacity documents are quoted as a flat fee based on your situation — so the cost of planning is clear before you weigh it against the cost of care. Elder law →
