Why It Matters

The three mistakes that ruin DIY deeds

The homestead signature

Married and it's your homestead? Your spouse must sign — even if they're not on title. Florida's constitution says so, and a deed without the joinder is voidable. Our builder gates on this automatically.

The copied-wrong legal description

The legal description must match the prior deed exactly — not the tax bill, not the listing. A character-level mismatch clouds title. We tell you exactly where to copy it from.

The doc-stamp surprise

Deed a mortgaged rental into your LLC "for free" and Florida taxes the loan balance as consideration. The builder runs the worksheet before you record, not after the bill arrives.

FAQ

Florida deeds — common questions

Lady Bird deed vs. living trust — which one?

For a single property with a simple "keep control now, kids get it later" goal, a Lady Bird deed is faster and cheaper. A trust adds incapacity management, staged distributions, and covers everything you own — many clients use both. If you're weighing them, that's a great use of the Estate Kit quiz or a quick call.

Will transferring my property trigger my mortgage's due-on-sale clause?

Transfers of your own residence into your trust, and Lady Bird transfers, sit inside federal Garn-St. Germain protections. Transfers of investment property to an LLC don't — most lenders don't act on estate-planning transfers, but you should know before you record, and the builder flags it.

Do I lose my title insurance when I move property to my LLC or trust?

Your owner's policy covers the named insured — a new owner entity generally isn't covered automatically. Ask your title company about an endorsement; the attorney tier walks through it with you.

Does the attorney tier include the recording fees?

The flat fee covers preparation, attorney involvement, and our handling of recording by mail. The county's recording fee and documentary stamps are government charges passed through at cost — we tell you the exact total before anything is mailed.
