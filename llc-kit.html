Why It Matters

Why the paperwork behind the filing is the point

The bank will ask

To open a company account you'll need more than the state filing: your EIN letter, operating agreement, and a banking resolution naming authorized signers. It's all in the kit.

Liability protection is earned

An LLC protects you only if you run it like a company — separate money, real records, documents that show the company is more than a name. Courts look straight at this.

Partners need rules

Multi-member companies without an operating agreement fall back on default statutes nobody read. Ownership percentages, buyouts, death, divorce — decided now, on paper, while everyone's friendly.

FAQ

Florida LLC — common questions

How much does it really cost to form a Florida LLC?

Florida's fees are $125 ($100 filing + $25 registered agent designation), plus $138.75 every year for the annual report. Our kit is $149 self-guided or $499 attorney-formed on top of the state's fees — with no processing markups, no teaser subscriptions, and every cost on this page.

Do I really need an operating agreement for a single-member LLC?

Florida doesn't require one — but your bank will ask for it, and it's core evidence that the company is real and separate from you when someone tries to pierce the veil. Florida law also treats single-member LLCs differently for creditor purposes (F.S. § 605.0503(4)), which the kit's agreement addresses head-on.

Should my rental property be in an LLC?

Very often yes — and frequently paired with a Florida Land Trust for privacy (the structure many investors use). The kit's real-estate purpose option adds tailored provisions, and our real estate practice can handle the deed work.

Can I switch from DIY to attorney help later?

Any time. Attorney review is available on every DIY package, and if you'd rather we take over the filing, call (888) 388-8445.

I'm not a U.S. citizen or resident — can I own a Florida LLC?

Yes. The formation is the easy part; the EIN-without-SSN process, ITIN questions, and FIRPTA withholding on real estate are where foreign owners get stuck. That's exactly what the Foreign Owner Package covers, attorney-guided end to end.
