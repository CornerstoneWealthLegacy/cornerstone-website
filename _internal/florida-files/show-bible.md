# THE FLORIDA FILES — Show Bible
*Chloe-vs-History format, translated for Truestead (law, PI, elder) + Simpson & Simpson (real estate). The recurring history-tainment series where Florida's wildest true stories explain the law that protects you today.*

## The format (per episode, 30–60s vertical)
1. **HOOK (0–3s):** the unbelievable Florida fact, stated to camera or on-screen: "In 1925, Florida sold the same swamp to eleven different people."
2. **THE STORY (3–35s):** fast, funny, visual — host narrates over period imagery (Higgsfield-generated historical b-roll: land-boom offices, cattle drives, 1920s courtrooms — our pipeline makes 'costume budget' obsolete).
3. **THE TURN (35–50s):** "…and THAT's why [the law that protects you] exists today." History becomes the client's protection.
4. **CTA (50–60s):** practice-area DM keyword + "Attorney advertising" microline where required.
*Comprehension laws apply: hook on screen in 3s, captions on everything, plain-English so-what at the end.*
