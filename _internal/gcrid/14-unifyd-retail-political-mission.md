# UN1FYD — The Retail × Political Mission
### The movement becomes a brand people wear, and the brand becomes a field operation
*Companion to: 05-unifyd-positioning.md (positioning stays LOCKED), geopolitical-playbook.md, 04-access-plan.md*

> **The 30-year read:** Every durable movement in modern history ran on two engines — a **retail engine** (something people buy and wear that declares who they are) and a **political engine** (message discipline, field organization, earned media, advance work). Campaigns have the second but die after election day because they never build the first. Brands have the first but stand for nothing durable because they never build the second. **UN1FYD runs both — a non-partisan prosperity movement with a retail line that funds it and a wearer base that IS the field program.** That's the thing no one has done: not cause-washing a t-shirt company, not merch-tabling a campaign — a movement where the product is membership and the customer is the precinct.

## PART 0 — What retail and politics each actually teach (the operating philosophy)

**From 30 years of retail:**
1. **People don't buy products; they buy who they become when they wear them.** Nike sells becoming an athlete. Patagonia sells becoming a steward. UN1FYD sells becoming a *builder* — someone on the side of building things, together, in the open.
2. **Scarcity beats inventory.** The drop model (Supreme) turned t-shirts into events. Movements should never have a "catalog" — they have *moments*.
3. **Margin is message-budget.** Every dollar of gross margin is a dollar of narrative you don't have to raise. Retail makes the movement self-funding — no donors, no strings, no one to answer to.
4. **Distribution is the product.** A worn shirt is a billboard the customer *paid you* to carry. Retail at movement-scale is earned media you manufacture.

**From 30 years of politics:**
1. **Message discipline wins.** One sentence, said ten thousand times, beats ten thousand sentences said once. UN1FYD's sentence exists and is locked: *"We build shared prosperity through transparent partnership."*
2. **Retail politics beats broadcast.** The handshake, the door, the local room — person-to-person is where belief transfers. Wearers + local chapters = the field program.
3. **Advance work makes moments.** No milestone is newsworthy by accident. Every GCRID signing, mission, or summit gets campaign-style advance: the visual, the line, the local validator, the follow-up.
4. **Non-partisanship is a position, not an absence of one.** In a polarized market, "we build, we don't fight" is the most differentiated political posture available — and the only one compatible with the Institute's neutrality.

## PART 2 — The retail engine

### 2.1 Product architecture (identity ladder, not a catalog)
| Tier | Product | Job | Price psychology |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flag** | The UN1FYD tee/hat — one mark, no clutter | Mass identity; the billboard | $28–38 impulse-identity zone |
| **Uniform** | Work jacket, canvas cap, builder's flannel — workwear language | The core wearer's daily kit; signals "builder" | $60–120; workwear = authenticity |
| **Drop** | Milestone capsules tied to real events ("Bogotá Mission 001," "Summit Series") | Scarcity, storytelling, press hook per drop | Limited runs; numbered; never restocked |
| **Artifact** | Premium: signed charter prints, challenge coins, founder-series pieces | Status for insiders/partners; gifts for dignitaries* | $150+; artifact, not apparel |

*\*Dignitary gifting: nominal-value items only, logged, and never to win official action — FCPA discipline applies even to hats.*

### 2.2 Design language
- **Workwear meets diplomacy:** duck canvas, embroidery, flag-patch construction — but the typography of an institution. Think Carhartt × the UN gift shop, executed like a streetwear house.
- The mark is **UN1FYD** — the "1" is the whole story (many made one). Never political colors; own a neutral palette (unbleached canvas, navy, safety-orange accent = construction, building).
- Every piece carries the line somewhere small: *"We build, together, in the open."*

### 2.3 Operations (zero-inventory until proven)
- **Phase 1 (now):** print-on-demand (Printful/Gelato) behind a Shopify or Netlify+Snipcart storefront — zero inventory risk, live in days. Reuse the existing GCRID/Truestead web + SEO engine for the storefront and article/content machine.
- **Phase 2 (after 3 profitable drops):** small-batch domestic runs for the Uniform tier (quality jump = margin jump), 300-unit drops.
- **Phase 3:** wholesale to aligned retail only (museum shops, airport international terminals, summit merch tables) — placement as positioning.
- **Entity:** UN1FYD retail runs as a DBA under the for-profit side (Steadhold/GCRID LLC line — NOT the Institute, NOT Truestead Law). Sales tax nexus, standard e-comm.

### 2.4 The retail P&L is the movement's war chest
Target blended gross margin ≥60%. Retail profit funds content, drops, and event advance — the movement never needs to fundraise, which is itself a story ("the movement that funds itself by building").

## PART 3 — The political engine (campaign mechanics, no candidate)

### 3.1 Message discipline (the "one sentence" rule)
- Master line: **"We build shared prosperity through transparent partnership."**
- Every piece of content, product page, and interview answer ladders to it. Build the **message box**: what we say / what we never say (see bright lines, Part 5).

### 3.2 The field program = the wearer base
- Every buyer is opt-in to **the Coalition list** (email/SMS). Segment: wearer → advocate → chapter lead → Coalition member (GCRID funnel).
- **Chapters ("Build Crews")** in diaspora-dense metros (Miami first — matches the Florida-inbound-first sequencing). A chapter is three things: a meetup, a service/build day, and a camera. Nothing else.
- Retail-politics cadence: chapter leads get the drop 48 hours early + a talking-points card with each capsule. You're arming surrogates the way a campaign arms precinct captains.

### 3.3 Earned-media advance work (the milestone machine)
For every GCRID/Truestead milestone (MOU celebration, trade mission, summit, consul-track step):
1. **The visual** — one designed image/clip (Higgsfield pipeline already exists) built *before* the event.
2. **The line** — one quotable sentence, pre-written.
3. **The validator** — one local third party (chamber, association, professor) quoted alongside.
4. **The capsule** — the milestone drop goes live the same hour. Press covers the story; the store converts the attention. **Attention → identity → list → Coalition → deal flow.**

### 3.4 The founder as candidate-without-a-race
Arthur front-and-center, campaign-style: stump speech (one, refined forever), town-hall format content, "listening tour" through diaspora communities. All the mechanics of a race with none of the filing — which keeps the appointed/advisory track (04-access-plan.md) fully open. The video machine (Truestead pipeline, Soul IDs) is the broadcast arm.

## PART 5 — Bright lines (additions to the locked list in 05-unifyd-positioning.md)

1. **Non-partisan is absolute.** No candidate merch, no election-adjacent drops, no partisan colorways, no endorsements — ever. One partisan drop contaminates the Institute permanently.
2. **Not a PAC, never money-in-politics.** UN1FYD retail revenue never flows to candidates, parties, or ballot committees. No campaign-finance surface area at all.
3. **Cause-marketing compliance:** if any drop advertises "X% supports the GCRID Institute," that's a **commercial co-venture** — register where required (FL Ch. 496 et al.), put the exact %/amount in the ad, written contract between LLC and Institute, arm's-length (inurement wall).
4. **FTC truth-in-advertising:** "Made in USA" only when it is; no puffery about outcomes ("this shirt builds peace" = no; "this drop celebrates the Bogotá mission" = yes).
5. **Peace = outcome, never product.** Same rule as the parent brief, applied to product copy.
6. **Firewall unchanged:** UN1FYD mark never on an MOU, filing, or certification. Celebrate, don't sign.
