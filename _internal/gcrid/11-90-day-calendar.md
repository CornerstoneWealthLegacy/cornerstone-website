# GCRID — 90-Day Daily Execution Calendar
### One focused action a day. Information to crush it. No wasted motion.

> **How to use:** one primary task per weekday (≈30–90 min — you have a firm to run). Weekends = light/optional catch-up. Many tasks kick off processes that run in the background (attorney filings, FIABCI, trademarks) — so you START them early and they mature while you keep moving. **Don't skip the gates** (entity + compliance) to chase the shiny stuff. Check the box, move on. Momentum compounds.
>
> **The 4 things that unlock everything (front-load these):** (1) nonprofit attorney engaged, (2) FIABCI joined, (3) SelectFlorida registered, (4) corridor #1 chosen. Everything else hangs off those.

## WEEK 1 — Foundation & filings *(goal: the machine is legally born)*
- **Mon** — Engage a **nonprofit + FCPA-aware attorney**; send them `02-entity-setup-brief.md` + the one-paragraph brief. *Why: the 501c3 is the prerequisite for UN status, grants, and credible MOUs. Start the clock — Form 1023 takes months.*

- **Wed** — Join **FIABCI-USA** (membership). *Why: FIABCI holds UN ECOSOC status — this is your shortcut into UN-adjacent rooms.*

- **Fri** — Confirm **CIPS** current; join **NAR Global** / local Global Business Council. *Why: your passport + warm intros to ~100 national associations.*

## The daily discipline (tape this up)
1. **One gate task before one glamour task.** Entity/compliance before summits/press.
2. **Start long-lead processes immediately** (attorney, FIABCI, trademarks, grants, UN status) — they mature in the background.
3. **One corridor, fully, beats ten half-corridors.** Depth first.
4. **Screen everyone (OFAC/PEP) before you engage them.** No exceptions.
5. **Every win becomes content (UN1FYD) and a Registry entry.** Compounding authority.
6. **Clean is the brand.** When in doubt, the answer is the transparent one.

*The 90 days turn the kit into motion. After Day 90 you'll have: the legal vehicle, FIABCI foothold, 1–2 signed MOUs, a vetted counsel network, a certified deal in the Registry, a grant in flight, a UN-status file open, and a summit on the calendar. That's no longer a plan — that's an institution.*
