# GCRID / Truestead — Video Language SOP

**The one rule that prevents brand damage:** decide the *tier* before you touch a video. Tier is determined by **whose authority the video sells**, not by topic or length.

| | **Tier A — Founder / Authority** | **Tier B — Volume Marketing** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Arthur on camera as GCRID founder / Truestead attorney: charter, principles, partner pitches, "why work with me," case-style explainers in his own voice | Top-of-funnel awareness content from the pipeline: tips, FAQs, statute explainers, listing/lifestyle, social hooks |
| **Source** | Real Arthur, recorded once, in **English** | HeyGen avatar + ElevenLabs (the Truestead Video Machine) |
| **Other languages** | **Subtitles ONLY.** Never re-recorded, never AI-dubbed. His face + voice *are* the authority asset. | **AI dub OK** (ElevenLabs multilingual). This is reach, not doctrine. |
| **First 3 seconds** | Subtitle card: *"English is the governing language of this message. Subtitles for accessibility."* | Same governing line in the description; in-video disclaimer card |
| **Optional add-on** | Local CIPS partner 10–15s intro/outro in their language ("I work with Arthur Simpson and GCRID in [country]…") — adds regional trust **without** touching Arthur's audio | Localized captions + partner reposts |
| **Never** | AI voice clone of Arthur · partner paraphrasing his message · machine dub of a founder video | — |

**Litmus test:** *"If a partner or client believed this was Arthur speaking in their language, would that help or mislead?"* If it would mislead → Tier A (subtitle only). If nobody cares whose voice it is → Tier B (dub fine).

---

## Why the split (the thing generic advice gets wrong)
Blanket "never AI-dub, subtitles only" would break your entire HeyGen/ElevenLabs pipeline. Blanket "AI-dub everything" would put a synthetic voice on your founder's face and quietly destroy the authority GCRID is built on. **The tier split keeps both:** the pipeline scales Tier B; Tier A protects the one thing that can't be regenerated — Arthur's credibility.

---

## Language priority (both tiers)
English (governing) → **Spanish → Portuguese → French → Arabic → Mandarin (Simplified).** Covers 80%+ of cross-border real-estate traffic. Add per-deal-flow demand after that.

---

## Production checklist

**Tier A (founder):**
1. Record once in English, calm/authoritative, clean audio.
2. Generate subtitle tracks (Descript / CapCut) for the 5 priority languages — **selectable** for site/email, **burned-in** for social.
3. Open with the governing subtitle card (3s).
4. (Optional) Append the partner local-context tag.
5. Description box: the one-line English governing/disclaimer + FL Bar line (`multilingual-governance.md`).
6. **Hard stop:** no ElevenLabs/clone on Arthur's audio. Subtitle only.

**Tier B (pipeline):**
1. Generate English master via HeyGen/ElevenLabs.
2. ElevenLabs multilingual dub for the priority languages (acceptable here).
3. Burned-in localized captions for social; governing line in description.
4. Route through Postiz; partners repost with localized captions.

---

## Email embed (both tiers)
Thumbnail → "Watch with subtitles in your language" → landing page with subtitle toggle. Never autoplay; never embed raw partner-paraphrased cuts of a Tier-A video.

## Governance footer (every description box)
> English is the governing language. Subtitles/translations provided for accessibility. Attorney advertising and general information, not legal advice; no attorney-client relationship is formed. Truestead Law, LLC · Arthur Simpson, Esq. · FL Bar No. 529265.
