# GCRID Counsel Network — Vetting Questionnaire & Process
### How you screen the local attorney in each corridor

> **Why this is mission-critical:** the local attorney *is* your fulfillment in that country. They paper the deal under their license (UPL barrier solved), they're your eyes on local law, and they carry your reputation. A weak, conflicted, or compromised counsel doesn't just lose a deal — it taints the coalition's "clean" brand and can create FCPA/sanctions exposure. **The gate is the product.** Vet ruthlessly.

---

## Engagement structure (read first — FL Bar compliance)
- Counsel join as **independent licensed attorneys**, NOT employees or partners of Truestead.
- Compensation = **referral fees where the jurisdiction permits, co-counsel splits between licensed lawyers, or their own fees billed directly** — **never** a split of Truestead's legal fee with a non-lawyer, and never a referral fee that violates the destination jurisdiction's rules. (FL Bar Rule 4-5.4; check local bar rules too.)
- Each relationship documented in a written **Counsel Network Agreement** (co-counsel / referral terms, confidentiality, anti-corruption, independence, no authority to bind GCRID/Truestead).

---

## PART A — The Questionnaire (counsel completes)

### 1. Licensing & Standing  *(gate — must pass)*
- Full legal name, firm, jurisdiction(s) of admission, bar/registration number(s).
- Years licensed. Current good standing? (Attach certificate / bar lookup.)
- Any past or pending disciplinary action, suspension, or sanction? (Details.)
- Professional liability / malpractice insurance — carrier, limits, current?

### 2. Practice Fit
- % of practice in **real estate / property** law (target ≥ 40%).
- Experience with **cross-border / foreign-buyer** transactions (describe 2–3).
- Experience with **entity formation, title/registry, escrow, and tax/withholding** on property deals.
- Languages spoken/written (and English working proficiency).

### 3. Transparency & AML capability
- Do you perform **client due diligence / KYC** and **source-of-funds** review? Describe your process.
- Familiarity with **beneficial-ownership** disclosure and **FATF**-aligned norms in your market.
- Do you screen parties against **sanctions lists**? Which?

### 4. Integrity & Risk  *(gate — red flags here can disqualify)*
- Any relationship to **government officials / politically exposed persons (PEPs)** that could create conflict? (Disclose.)
- Have you ever been asked to facilitate a payment to an official? How did you handle it?
- Will you sign GCRID's **anti-corruption commitment** (no bribes/facilitation payments; FCPA + local law)?
- Any business interests that conflict with neutral representation in cross-border deals?

### 5. Independence & Conflicts
- Are you free of exclusive ties to a single developer/brokerage that would bias referrals?
- Conflict-check process before taking a GCRID-referred matter.

### 6. References & Capacity
- 3 professional references (one a foreign counterpart or institutional client if possible).
- Current capacity to take on referred matters; typical turnaround.
- Fee structure for the matters GCRID would refer.

## PART B — Scoring (you/the Institute complete)

Score 1–5 per dimension; **any gate failure = automatic decline regardless of total.**

| Dimension | Weight | Gate? |
|---|---|---|
| Licensing & good standing | — | **GATE** (must be clean) |
| Integrity / no corruption exposure | — | **GATE** (red flag = stop) |
| Sanctions/PEP exposure clear | — | **GATE** (OFAC-screen the attorney + firm) |
| Real-estate practice depth | 25% | |
| Cross-border experience | 20% | |
| AML/KYC capability | 20% | |
| Independence / low conflict | 15% | |
| Language / communication | 10% | |
| References / capacity | 10% | |

**Decision bands:** ≥ 4.0 avg + all gates clear → admit. 3.0–3.9 → conditional (probationary first matter, supervised). < 3.0 or any gate fail → decline.

## PART C — Process (run for every candidate)
1. **Source** — IBA / UIA directories, NAR bilateral association, CIPS network, embassy referrals, FIABCI contacts.
2. **Screen** — OFAC/sanctions/PEP check on the attorney AND firm BEFORE the interview.
3. **Questionnaire** — they complete Part A; attach bar certificate + insurance proof.
4. **Verify** — independently confirm bar standing; call references; check for discipline.
5. **Interview** — probe the integrity questions live (Section 4 answers matter most).
6. **Score** — Part B; gates first.
7. **Probation** — first 1–2 matters supervised/co-counseled before full network status.
8. **Agreement** — sign the Counsel Network Agreement (independence, anti-corruption, confidentiality, compensation).
9. **Re-vet annually** — re-screen sanctions/standing; review matter quality.

---

## RED FLAGS — decline or escalate
- Not in current good standing, or undisclosed discipline.
- Evasive or normalizing answers on facilitation payments / official "fees."
- Undisclosed PEP/government ties surfacing in screening.
- Sanctions/adverse-media hit on attorney or firm.
- No malpractice insurance.
- Pushes to skip KYC/source-of-funds "to move faster."
- Exclusive financial tie to one developer steering all referrals.

---

## The principle
**You are not hiring lawyers; you are extending your reputation into a country.** The counsel who proudly *refuses* the facilitation payment is worth more than the one with the biggest book of business. Hire for integrity first, capability second — in that order, every time.
