# GCRID Global Summit — Run-of-Show & Sponsorship Deck
### The annual credentialing event that manufactures authority

> **Why the summit matters more than it looks:** a summit is the fastest way to *manufacture institutional authority*. The moment GCRID convenes national authorities, attorneys, and investors under one banner, you ARE the convener — the hub ChatGPT said you must become. It also produces the case-study moments UN1FYD amplifies and the MOUs the Institute signs. Start small; the *photo of the room* is the asset.

---

## PART 1 — Summit concept

**Name:** The GCRID Global Summit — *Building Transparent, Investable Markets.*
**Positioning:** a working convening of national real-estate authorities, cross-border attorneys, developers, lenders, and investors around transparency, property rights, and clean FDI. **Institutional, not promotional.** (UN1FYD amplifies it externally; the Summit itself stays neutral/Institute-branded.)
**V1 reality check:** your first summit can be **50–150 people, one day, one city** (Miami is the natural LATAM gateway venue). A focused room with 2–3 foreign delegations beats an empty grand ballroom. Scale annually.

**Objectives (in priority order):**
1. Sign / announce **MOUs** with 1–3 national authorities (the headline).
2. Launch/showcase the **GCRID Standard** + first Registry entries.
3. Recruit **Counsel Network + members** (the room is your pipeline).
4. Produce **media/authority content** (UN1FYD reel, founder keynote, signing photos).

## PART 2 — Run-of-show (one-day V1)

| Time | Segment | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 8:30 | Registration + networking breakfast | Warm the room; badge = belonging |
| 9:15 | **Founder keynote** (Arthur) | The vision: transparent, investable markets; "why now" |
| 9:45 | **The GCRID Standard** unveiling | The product nations want — Transaction/Firm/Market tiers |
| 10:15 | Panel: *Clean capital across borders* | Foreign authority reps + attorneys; you moderate (= you're the hub) |
| 11:00 | Break + Registry demo | Show the public Registry live |
| 11:20 | Country spotlights (5–8 min each) | Each delegation presents its market = they feel ownership |
| 12:00 | **MOU signing ceremony** | THE photo. Authorities sign onto the Charter |
| 12:30 | Lunch + matchmaking | Curated investor↔market intros (deal flow) |
| 2:00 | Workshop: *Certifying your first transaction* | Practitioners → pipeline |
| 3:00 | Honorary recognitions / Counsel Network induction | Status + loyalty |
| 3:30 | Closing + next-summit announcement | Momentum; commitments |
| 6:00 | **Founders' reception** (invite-only) | Where the real relationships + next MOUs form |

**Run-of-show discipline:** the signing ceremony and the curated matchmaking are the two non-negotiable segments — one makes authority, the other makes revenue. Everything else is supporting cast.

## PART 3 — Sponsorship deck (slide-by-slide outline)

Build this as a clean institutional deck (navy/gold, matches the Charter page). Slides:

1. **Cover** — GCRID Global Summit, dates, city, tagline.
2. **The opportunity** — $X trillion in cross-border real estate; transparency is the unmet need (cite FATF/FinCEN pressure).
3. **The problem** — opaque ownership, weak registries, capital that can't enter cleanly.
4. **GCRID** — the coalition + the Standard + the Registry (one slide, simple).
5. **Who's in the room** — national authorities, attorneys, developers, lenders, investors (logos/flags as they commit).
6. **Why sponsor** — access to vetted cross-border decision-makers + government delegations; association with the transparency standard.
7. **Sponsorship tiers** (below).
8. **Audience profile** — titles, countries, deal sizes.
9. **Past proof** (year 2+) — MOUs signed, deals certified, media reach.
10. **The ask + contact** — tiers, deadline, sponsor liaison.

### Sponsorship tiers (illustrative — price to your market)
| Tier | Includes | Indicative |
|---|---|---|
| **Founding Partner** | Top billing, keynote slot, MOU-ceremony presence, year-round Registry placement, reception co-host | $$$$ |
| **Corridor Sponsor** | Country-spotlight sponsorship, panel seat, booth | $$$ |
| **Standard Sponsor** | Logo on Standard/Registry materials, workshop slot | $$ |
| **Supporting** | Logo, tickets, networking access | $ |
| **In-kind** | Venue, AV, translation, hospitality (preserve cash) | — |

**Clean-money rule:** sponsors get OFAC/sanctions-screened too. A tainted sponsor logo on a transparency summit is fatal. Vet them like members.

## PART 4 — Economics & logistics (V1)
- **Revenue:** sponsorships + delegation/ticket fees + (year 2+) certification sales on-site.
- **Cost control:** in-kind venue (a partner law firm, university, or chamber of commerce), volunteer/committee labor, livestream instead of flying everyone.
- **Run it through the Institute** (nonprofit) for credibility + any grant eligibility; keep sponsorship accounting clean and arm's-length from the LLC.
- **Translation:** the multilingual assets + on-site interpretation for delegations (Tier-A founder content subtitled, not dubbed).

---

## The strategic point
The summit is where **the whole stack converges**: the Charter gets signed (MOUs), the Standard gets adopted, the Registry gets populated, the Counsel Network gets inducted, UN1FYD gets its content, and sponsors fund it. **One room, once a year, that makes you the convener.** Start at 100 people; in three years it's the event a ministry flies to.
