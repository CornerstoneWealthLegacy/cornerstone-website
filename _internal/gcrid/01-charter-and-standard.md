# GCRID Charter & The GCRID Standard v1
### The founding document nations and partners sign onto

> This is the centerpiece. The **Charter** states who GCRID is and what it stands for (governments sign MOUs *to* this). The **Standard** is the measurable certification — what makes a transaction, firm, or market "GCRID-compliant." The Standard is the product nations and the UN actually want, because it gives them a credential to prove their market is clean and investable.

---

## PART A — THE GCRID CHARTER

**Name:** The Global Coalition for Real Estate, Investment & Development (GCRID).
**Form:** Convened by the GCRID Institute (a U.S. nonprofit). Membership coalition of vetted agents, attorneys, lenders, developers, and national real-estate authorities.

### Preamble
GCRID exists to advance **transparent, lawful, and investable real-estate markets worldwide** — so that capital flows cleanly across borders, property rights are secure, and nations attract foreign investment without importing money-laundering or corruption.

### Founding Principles (the 7 pillars)
1. **Transparency** — beneficial ownership and source-of-funds are knowable in every cross-border transaction.
2. **Legality** — every transaction is papered by counsel licensed in the relevant jurisdiction.
3. **Anti-corruption** — zero tolerance for bribery, facilitation payments, or sanctioned counterparties (FCPA/OFAC/UN-aligned).
4. **Property rights** — secure, recorded, enforceable title is the foundation of investable markets.
5. **Capacity** — GCRID builds local capability; it does not replace local professionals.
6. **Neutrality** — GCRID is non-partisan and non-political; it serves markets and the rule of law, not factions.
7. **Mutual benefit** — corridors are two-way; host nations gain clean FDI and diaspora capital, not extraction.

### Membership tiers (vetted; you are the gatekeeper)
- **Founding / National Authority** — a country's real-estate authority or association signs an MOU adopting the Standard.
- **Counsel Network** — one+ locally-licensed attorney per jurisdiction (co-counsel/referral; never legal-fee-split per FL Bar 4-5.4).
- **Accredited Practitioner** — agents/brokers/developers who pass vetting + training.
- **Affiliate** — lenders, title/escrow, compliance vendors.

### Governance
- Independent board (mix of real-estate, legal, AML/compliance, development-finance backgrounds) — required for nonprofit credibility and grant eligibility.
- Standards Committee owns the Standard; revised annually.
- Public Charter; transparent membership criteria; published code of conduct.

### What a nation signs (the MOU artifact)
A Memorandum of Understanding in which the national authority: (a) recognizes the GCRID Standard, (b) collaborates on capacity-building/transparency, (c) designates a liaison, (d) participates in the corridor. *This is the "the country asked us to come" proof.* Non-binding, non-financial, FCPA-clean.

## PART B — THE GCRID STANDARD v1
### The measurable certification (grounded in FATF + FinCEN, so it's credible to governments)

The Standard certifies three things: **a Transaction**, **a Firm/Practitioner**, or **a National Market**. Each has criteria. Certification is the revenue + the authority engine.

### B1 — Transaction Standard (per-deal certification)
A cross-border transaction is **GCRID-Certified** when it meets all of:
1. **Identity (CDD)** — verified identity of all parties (FATF R.10).
2. **Beneficial ownership** — true human owners behind any entity disclosed and recorded (FATF R.24/25; aligns with U.S. FinCEN residential real-estate rule, eff. Dec 2025).
3. **Source of funds** — documented, plausible, screened.
4. **Sanctions screening** — all parties cleared against OFAC/EU/UN lists.
5. **Licensed counsel both sides** — competent local + U.S. counsel papered the deal.
6. **Title & escrow** — recorded title; funds through regulated escrow/title.
7. **Disclosure** — material facts and cross-border tax/withholding (e.g., FIRPTA) addressed.
8. **Record retention** — file retained to defend the transaction.

### B2 — Practitioner / Firm Standard (membership certification)
A firm is **GCRID-Accredited** when it: adopts a written AML/anti-corruption policy; trains staff on the Transaction Standard; maintains sanctions-screening; carries appropriate licensure/insurance; and submits to periodic review.

### B3 — National Market Standard (the government-facing prize)
A market earns a **GCRID Market Transparency Tier (Bronze/Silver/Gold)** based on: registry digitization & accessibility; beneficial-ownership regime; title security/enforceability; AML supervision of real-estate professionals; FDI openness; dispute resolution. *A government can cite its tier to attract clean capital — which is exactly why they'll engage you to raise it.*

### Certification mechanics
- **Self-assessment → independent review → certificate + registry listing.**
- Certificates are time-bound (annual renewal = recurring revenue).
- Public GCRID Registry of certified transactions/firms/markets = the network effect + trust signal.

## The strategic point
The **Charter** is what makes you *invitable* (neutral, principled, government-friendly). The **Standard** is what makes you *needed* (a credential nations and firms pay for and point to). Together they convert "American firm wants deals" into "the body that certifies clean, investable markets." Build the Standard well and governments compete for a higher tier — and every certified deal runs through the Truestead engine underneath.
