# GCRID — This-Month Onboarding Pack
### The concrete moves to make NOW (start the flywheel)

> Real organizations, real steps. Verify current fees/process on each org's site — they change. Nothing here costs much; the point is to start *this month* so authority begins compounding.

---

## MOVE 1 — Join FIABCI (the UN-seat shortcut)
**Why:** FIABCI (International Real Estate Federation) holds **UN ECOSOC consultative status**. Membership → activity → leadership lets you borrow that UN access instead of waiting 1–2 years for your own.
**Do this month:**
1. Join via **FIABCI-USA** (the U.S. chapter) — individual/professional membership.
2. Attend the next FIABCI-USA event / global congress (calendar on fiabci.org).
3. Volunteer for a **committee** (e.g., a working group touching transparency, cross-border, or developing markets) — that's the climb path.
4. Introduce GCRID's mission (transparency/standards) — natural alignment; you become "the standards person."
**Outcome:** warm path into UN-adjacent rooms within months.

## MOVE 2 — Get on SelectFlorida (state trade missions)
**Why:** Official Florida international trade missions = credentialed government-banner access to foreign authorities, FARA-safe.
**Do this month:**
1. Register Truestead/GCRID as a Florida business with **SelectFlorida** (the state's international trade/economic-dev arm).
2. Ask their trade team about the **upcoming mission calendar**; target one to a priority corridor (Colombia / Brazil / UAE).
3. Also register with the **U.S. Commercial Service** (Dept. of Commerce, trade.gov) for **Gold Key** matchmaking.
**Outcome:** a calendar of government-led entries to your corridors.

## MOVE 3 — Activate NAR Global / CIPS network
**Why:** NAR has bilateral cooperating agreements with ~100 national real-estate associations; CIPS is your passport.
**Do this month:**
1. Confirm CIPS designation current; join **NAR Global** / a local Global Business Council.
2. Request introductions to corridor #1's national real-estate association via NAR's bilateral-partner list.
3. Identify 2–3 CIPS designees in each corridor as first **Counsel/partner-network** leads.
**Outcome:** warm intros to each corridor's real-estate authority.

## MOVE 4 — File the entity + protect the marks (cross-ref 02-entity-setup-brief)
**Do this month:**
1. Engage a **nonprofit + FCPA-aware attorney**; start the **GCRID Institute (501c3)** formation (Form 1023 in motion).
2. Adopt the 5 compliance policies (anti-corruption, OFAC, AML, COI, data).
3. **Trademark**: GCRID, the GCRID Standard mark, and **UN1FYD** (separately — firewall).
**Outcome:** the legal vehicle that can take UN status, grants, and MOUs exists.

## MOVE 5 — Open corridor #1 (the proof)
**Do this month (pick Colombia or wherever you have the warmest tie):**
1. OFAC/sanctions-screen the country + any first counterparties.
2. Recruit + vet **one local attorney** (via IBA, NAR bilateral assoc, or CIPS).
3. Send the **Institute outreach** (capacity-building / transparency MOU) to the national association — use the multilingual email template.
4. Aim to sign the **first MOU** (06-mou-template) within the quarter.
**Outcome:** the first signed "nation partnered with us" artifact + first deal corridor.

## What NOT to do this month
- Don't pay for "UN access" consultants or pay-to-play awards — FIABCI + a real ECOSOC application are the legitimate routes.
- Don't sign anything financial/binding with a government yet (FCPA/procurement). MOUs only.
- Don't chase 10 countries. One corridor, signed, beats ten conversations.
- Don't fuse UN1FYD into the Institute's government-facing materials.
