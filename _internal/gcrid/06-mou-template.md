# GCRID — Memorandum of Understanding (Template)
### The "the nation asked us to come" instrument

> **What this is:** a non-binding, no-money-changes-hands cooperation memorandum between the **GCRID Institute** and a national/regional real-estate authority or association. It creates the *relationship and the public artifact* — not legal obligations. It is the document that turns "we'd like access" into "we have a signed partnership."
>
> **⚠ Before any signing:** have counsel review for the specific jurisdiction; run OFAC/sanctions screening on the counterparty and country; confirm the signatory has authority. This template is a drafting starting point, not legal advice. Keep it **strictly non-binding and non-financial** so it raises no FCPA, procurement, or FARA issues.

---

## MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

**between**

**The GCRID Institute** — a nonprofit organization advancing transparent, lawful, and investable real-estate markets *(the "Institute")*

**and**

**[NAME OF NATIONAL/REGIONAL REAL-ESTATE AUTHORITY OR ASSOCIATION]** *(the "Authority")*

*(each a "Party," together the "Parties")*

---

### 1. Purpose & Shared Vision
The Parties share a commitment to **transparent, lawful, and investable real-estate markets** that attract clean foreign and diaspora investment while safeguarding property rights and the rule of law. This Memorandum records their intent to cooperate toward those ends. It reflects partnership and shared prosperity — not the transfer of authority, funds, or obligations.

### 2. Areas of Cooperation
The Parties intend to collaborate, as mutually agreed and resources permit, on:
1. **Capacity building** — education and training for real-estate professionals, attorneys, and officials on transparent transaction standards.
2. **The GCRID Standard** — sharing and, where appropriate, recognizing the GCRID Standard for transparent cross-border transactions and market transparency tiers.
3. **Transparency & anti-money-laundering** — promoting beneficial-ownership disclosure, source-of-funds practices, and FATF-aligned norms in real estate.
4. **Investment facilitation** — supporting lawful, transparent channels for foreign and diaspora investment into [COUNTRY]'s real-estate market, and for [COUNTRY] investors abroad.
5. **Knowledge exchange** — research, data, best practices, and participation in GCRID and Authority events/summits.

### 3. Nature of This Memorandum (Non-Binding)
3.1 This Memorandum is a **statement of intent only**. It is **not legally binding**, creates **no enforceable obligations**, and is **not a contract, joint venture, partnership, or agency** between the Parties.
3.2 It imposes **no financial obligation** on either Party. Each Party bears its own costs. No funds, fees, or things of value are exchanged under this Memorandum.
3.3 Neither Party may bind, represent, or act on behalf of the other. Neither Party is the agent of the other.

### 4. Independence, Legality & Professional Practice
4.1 Each Party acts independently and in compliance with all applicable laws of its jurisdiction.
4.2 Nothing herein constitutes the practice of law in [COUNTRY]. Any legal services in [COUNTRY] will be provided solely by attorneys licensed in [COUNTRY]; the Institute and its U.S. affiliates do not practice [COUNTRY] law.
4.3 The Institute is a nonprofit; any commercial real-estate or legal services referenced are provided by independent licensed professionals, not by the Institute.

### 5. Anti-Corruption & Sanctions (Express)
5.1 The Parties will conduct all activities in compliance with applicable anti-corruption laws, including the **U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act** and applicable anti-bribery laws of [COUNTRY].
5.2 No Party will offer, give, or accept any bribe, kickback, or improper payment in connection with this Memorandum.
5.3 Activities will comply with applicable **economic sanctions and export controls** (including U.S. OFAC, and UN/EU measures). The Parties will not engage sanctioned persons or jurisdictions.

### 6. No Exclusivity; No Endorsement
This Memorandum is non-exclusive. It does not constitute a government endorsement, procurement, or preferential treatment of any commercial entity, and confers no exclusive rights on the Institute or any affiliate.

### 7. Confidentiality & Data
Each Party will protect non-public information shared under this Memorandum and handle any personal data in accordance with applicable data-protection laws.

### 8. Liaisons
Each Party designates a liaison to coordinate activities:
- Institute: [NAME, TITLE, CONTACT]
- Authority: [NAME, TITLE, CONTACT]

### 9. Term & Termination
This Memorandum takes effect on the last signature date and continues for **[24] months**, renewable by mutual written agreement. Either Party may terminate at any time on **[30] days'** written notice, without liability.

### 10. Governing Language
This Memorandum is executed in **English**, the governing language. Any translation is provided for accessibility only; in case of discrepancy, the **English version controls**.

### 11. Public Communication
Either Party may announce the existence of this cooperation. Joint public statements require mutual consent. *(Note: GCRID Institute branding only — UN1FYD movement branding does not appear on this instrument.)*

## Drafting notes (delete before sending)
- Keep it **non-binding + non-financial** — that's what keeps it FCPA/procurement/FARA-safe and easy for a ministry's legal team to approve fast.
- If the Authority is a **government body**, expect their legal review to *want* §§3, 5, 6 — lead with them; they're features.
- Localize for reference (English governs) into the corridor language; attach the localized governing/disclaimer block from `multilingual-governance.md`.
- This MOU is signed by the **Institute** (neutral), never UN1FYD, never the LLC.
