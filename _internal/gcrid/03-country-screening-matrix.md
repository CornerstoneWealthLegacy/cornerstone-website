# GCRID — Country Screening Matrix & First 3 Corridors

> How to choose where to open corridors — scored, not by gut. Run every candidate through this before committing counsel or capital.

---

## The scoring model (score each 1–5, apply weight)

| Dimension | Weight | 5 = ideal | 1 = avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sanctions/OFAC clear** | GATE | Fully clear | Sanctioned → hard NO (disqualifies regardless of other scores) |
| **Rule of law / title security** | 20% | Strong recorded title, enforceable | Insecure, contested title |
| **FDI appetite (inbound to U.S. + into-country)** | 20% | Actively courts foreign capital | Closed/hostile to foreign buyers |
| **Diaspora / warm ties to Florida** | 15% | Large FL diaspora, existing relationships | None |
| **Corruption risk (inverse)** | 15% | Low (clean to work in) | High (FCPA exposure) |
| **Market size / deal value** | 10% | Large cross-border RE flow | Thin |
| **Language/cultural fit (assets built)** | 10% | Spanish/Portuguese/French (you have rails) | No coverage |
| **Ease of local counsel recruitment** | 10% | Strong bar, English-capable lawyers | Hard to vet |

**Rule:** sanctions is a *gate*, not a score — any OFAC/EU/UN hit = automatic no. Corruption risk doesn't disqualify, but high-risk markets require the strictest FCPA posture and the cleanest local counsel.

## Recommended first 3 corridors (strategic logic)

**Anchor your launch in Latin America + Florida — it's where your gravity already is** (diaspora, air links, Spanish/Portuguese assets, and Florida's role as *the* U.S. entry point for LATAM capital). Then add one premium global node.

1. **Colombia** — your **inbound flagship.** Big, warm, Spanish, FDI-active, real FL diaspora. Easiest "first MOU" + steady deal flow. Proves the model.
2. **Brazil** — your **scale play.** Largest LATAM market, Portuguese rails already built, strong FL presence, development upside for Phase 3.
3. **UAE (Dubai)** — your **premium / outbound node + global stage.** Where the world's luxury and capital concentrate; the transparency/clean-coalition pitch lands hard in a market under AML scrutiny, and Dubai's summit/conference circuit is a megaphone for GCRID's authority.

> Why this trio: two deep, warm, fast-revenue LATAM corridors that *fund and prove* the model, plus one high-prestige global node that *amplifies authority*. **Adjust corridor #1–3 to wherever Arthur has real relationships** — a warm intro beats a high score every time.

## Per-corridor launch steps (repeat)
1. Sanctions/OFAC clear the country + first counterparties.
2. Recruit + vet **one local attorney** (IBA/UIA networks, NAR bilateral association, CIPS contacts).
3. Enter as the **Institute** — offer capacity-building / a transparency MOU to the national real-estate association or authority.
4. Sign the MOU (non-binding, FCPA-clean).
5. Open the two-way corridor; Truestead + local counsel paper/close.
6. Publish the case study → recruits the next 3.
