The GCRID Charter

Transparent, lawful, and investable real-estate markets — for every nation.

GCRID is a coalition of vetted professionals and national authorities advancing clean cross-border investment, secure property rights, and shared prosperity through partnership.

Preamble

Why we exist

Capital should flow across borders cleanly — building opportunity without importing corruption. GCRID convenes agents, attorneys, lenders, developers, and national real-estate authorities around a shared standard of transparency, so markets become safer to invest in and stronger to build on.

The Charter

The Seven Pillars

Every member and partner upholds these. They are the conditions of membership and the basis of every Memorandum of Understanding.

01

Transparency

Beneficial ownership and source of funds are knowable in every cross-border transaction.

02

Legality

Every transaction is papered by counsel licensed in the relevant jurisdiction.

03

Anti-Corruption

Zero tolerance for bribery, facilitation payments, or sanctioned counterparties.

04

Property Rights

Secure, recorded, enforceable title is the foundation of investable markets.

05

Capacity

We build local capability; we never replace local professionals.

06

Neutrality

Non-partisan and non-political — we serve markets and the rule of law, not factions.

07

Mutual Benefit

Corridors are two-way: host nations gain clean investment, not extraction.

The GCRID Standard

What we certify

A measurable standard, aligned with international transparency norms (FATF / beneficial-ownership). It gives professionals and nations a credential that proves a transaction — or a market — is clean and investable.

Certification I

Transaction

  • Verified identity (CDD)
  • Beneficial ownership disclosed
  • Source of funds documented
  • Sanctions screened
  • Licensed counsel both sides
  • Recorded title & regulated escrow
Certification II

Firm / Practitioner

  • Written AML & anti-corruption policy
  • Trained on the Standard
  • Sanctions screening in place
  • Proper licensure & insurance
  • Periodic independent review
Certification III

National Market

  • Registry digitization & access
  • Beneficial-ownership regime
  • Title security & enforceability
  • AML supervision of professionals
  • FDI openness & dispute resolution
