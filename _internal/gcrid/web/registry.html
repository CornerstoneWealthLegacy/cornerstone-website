How certification works

01

Apply

Submit a transaction, firm, or market for review against the GCRID Standard.

02

Review

Independent review verifies the criteria and screens all parties.

03

Certify

On passing, a certificate issues and the entry joins the public Registry.

04

Renew

Certifications are time-bound and renewed annually to stay current.

GCRID
The GCRID Registry is maintained by the GCRID Institute. Listings indicate conformance with the GCRID Standard at the time of certification.
