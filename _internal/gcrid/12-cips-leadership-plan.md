# CIPS Expansion & Leadership Plan
### Turn the CIPS designation into an official NAR leadership seat that supercharges GCRID

> **The key insight (researched from nar.realtor):** the **NAR Global Ambassador** is a member *appointed by the NAR President to serve as the official U.S. liaison to a specific country/region's real-estate associations.* That is — almost exactly — the GCRID corridor role, but with NAR's institutional authority behind you. Becoming the Global Ambassador to your corridor (e.g., Colombia/Andean LatAm) means you don't *ask* for access to that nation's real-estate authority — you're NAR's *designated bridge* to it. **This is the single highest-leverage credential move available to you.**

---

## The CIPS leadership ladder (climb in order)

### Rung 0 — Lock the CIPS designation *(if not already done)*
- **Requirement:** 2 core courses ("Global Real Estate: Local Markets" + "Global Real Estate: Transaction Tools") + **3 electives** (pick regional electives matching your corridors — e.g., **The Americas & International Real Estate**) + **100 points of international experience** + application.
- **Directive:** if you hold CIPS, confirm it's current and your CIPS Network profile is complete. If not, register for the next **CIPS Institute** (the courses are often offered as a one-week intensive) and knock out all 5 courses at once. As an attorney with international/cross-border work, your 100 experience points are easy to document.
- **Why first:** CIPS is the prerequisite credibility for every rung above.

### Rung 1 — Chair a local/state Global Business Council *(fastest leadership win — weeks, not years)*
- Most state/large-local REALTOR® associations (Florida Realtors, your local board) have a **Global Business Council** or international committee.
- **Directive:** email your local board + Florida Realtors' global staff today; join the Global Business Council, then volunteer to **chair or vice-chair** it. These seats are frequently undersubscribed — an active CIPS attorney is exactly who they want.
- **Payoff:** an immediate, real "leader in global real estate" title + a platform + the relationships that nominate you upward. This is your launchpad.

### Rung 2 — File the NAR Global "Expression of Interest" *(do this week — it's always open)*
- NAR has a standing **Expression of Interest** form to be notified when Global Ambassador/Coordinator slots in your countries open.
- **Directive:** file the EOI now, listing **your corridor countries** (Colombia / the Andean or South America grouping). Then email **Karen Williams, NAR Director of Global & Commercial Strategy — kwilliams@nar.realtor** — introduce yourself as a CIPS attorney building cross-border practice, express interest in the Global Ambassador role for [your corridor], and ask when that grouping next terms off.
- **Why now:** appointments run an annual cycle; getting on staff's radar early is half the game.

### Rung 3 — Win the Global Ambassador appointment *(the prize)*
- **What it is:** U.S.-based NAR member, **appointed by the NAR President**, serving as liaison to a country grouping's real-estate professionals. **4-year term.** Six regions each have a **Global Coordinator**; country groupings within each have **Global Ambassadors**.
- **Annual timeline:** Call for Applications ~**Q2** (open ~30 days) → interviews **June/July** → appointment by the NAR President **Sept/Oct**.
- **Directive:** apply in the next Q2 window for the **country grouping that matches GCRID corridor #1.** Tailor the application around your differentiator: *a Florida attorney + CIPS who can not only refer but paper and close cross-border deals, and who is building a transparency-standards coalition.* That's a stronger candidacy than a typical agent applicant.
- **Strategic alignment:** make your **GCRID corridor #1 = your Global Ambassador country.** The two roles then reinforce each other — NAR authority opens the door, GCRID/Truestead closes the deal.

### Rung 4 — Global Coordinator → NAR committee leadership *(years 2–4)*
- After serving as Ambassador, climb to **Global Coordinator** (one of 6 regions) and seek seats on NAR's global committees (Global Coordinating Committee, Global Business & Alliances).
- **Directive:** during your Ambassador term, over-deliver (events, bilateral MOUs, member engagement) so you're the obvious Coordinator pick. Document everything for the nomination.

### Rung 5 (parallel) — FIABCI leadership
- Run the **FIABCI** climb (the federation with **UN ECOSOC status**) alongside the NAR track. NAR Global + FIABCI leadership together make you a genuine global figure, not a local agent. *(See 07-onboarding-pack + geopolitical-playbook.)*

## How this turbo-charges GCRID (the compounding)
- **Global Ambassador = official bridge** to your corridor's real-estate authority → the GCRID MOU outreach now comes from an *NAR-appointed liaison*, not a cold law firm. Doors open.
- **NAR Global Alliances** (~100 bilateral partner associations) become your warm-intro map for every future corridor.
- **The titles stack:** "Florida Attorney · CIPS · NAR Global Ambassador to [Country] · Founder, GCRID Institute" — that signature is institutional authority that earns trade-mission seats, embassy meetings, and the honorary-consul path.
