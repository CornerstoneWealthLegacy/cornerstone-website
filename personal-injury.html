First Things First

What to do after an accident in Florida.

1

Get medical care

See a doctor — even if you feel "okay." Some injuries surface days later, and a treatment record matters.

2

Document everything

Photos, names, the police or incident report, and what was said. Keep it.

3

Don't sign yet

An early settlement check or recorded statement can cost you. Talk to a lawyer before you sign or give a statement.

4

Call us

A free case review costs you nothing and starts the clock in your favor, not theirs.

How It Works

Straightforward, and on your side.

You pay nothing up front. Personal injury cases are handled on a contingency fee — there's no attorney's fee unless we obtain a recovery for you. If there is no recovery, you owe no attorney's fee. (As in any case, clients may be responsible for costs, and those terms are explained in writing before you sign.)

One firm. The right team. When your matter calls for litigation, Truestead Law brings in experienced Florida trial counsel as co-counsel and remains responsible for your case alongside them. You're told in writing who is working on your matter, and associating co-counsel does not increase your total fee. You always have one firm answering to you — ours.

We move quickly. Florida deadlines are real, and evidence fades. The sooner we start, the more we can protect.

Whether you have a claim, and what any claim may be worth, can only be evaluated by an attorney after reviewing your specific facts. We do not predict outcomes or estimate value online. Every case is different, and prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.
