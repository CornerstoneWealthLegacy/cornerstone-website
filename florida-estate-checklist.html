The Florida Estate Planning Checklist

The documents and decisions every Florida adult should have in place. Check off what you've done — and see where the gaps are.

📄 The core documents

Last Will & Testament
Names who inherits, your personal representative, and a guardian for minor children.
Revocable Living Trust
Keeps your home and accounts out of Florida probate — private, faster, less costly.
Durable Power of Attorney
Lets someone you trust handle finances if you can't.
Health Care Surrogate
Names who makes medical decisions for you.
Living Will
Puts your end-of-life wishes in writing so your family doesn't have to guess.

🏠 Your Florida home & assets

Confirm how your home is titled
Homestead, joint ownership, a lady bird deed, or a trust — each passes differently in Florida.
Fund your trust (if you have one)
Re-title your home and accounts into the trust — an unfunded trust avoids nothing.
Review beneficiary designations
Retirement accounts & life insurance pass by designation — they override your will.

👪 People & decisions

Name a guardian for minor children
Without one, a Florida court decides who raises them.
Choose your personal representative & agents
Make sure they're willing, able, and Florida-eligible.
Update after big life events
Marriage, divorce, a new child, a move to Florida, or a major asset change.

🌴 New to Florida or planning ahead?

Update an out-of-state plan
Florida's homestead and execution rules are unique — old documents may not fit.
Think about long-term care
Powers of attorney, health directives, and Medicaid planning protect you and your savings.
Sign correctly
Florida requires two witnesses; a self-proving affidavit makes probate smoother.
