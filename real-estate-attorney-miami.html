Deeds & Property Transfers in Miami

Moving a Miami property to family, into a trust, or out of a deceased owner's name takes the right deed prepared correctly. We draft and record warranty deeds, quitclaim deeds, and enhanced life estate ("Lady Bird") deeds for Miami-Dade County property, choosing the form that fits your goal — transferring to loved ones, funding a revocable living trust, or keeping the home out of probate. Documentary stamp tax and recording fees apply, and we handle the recording with the Miami-Dade County Clerk.

Miami Real Estate FAQs

What real estate services does Truestead offer in Miami?

For Miami owners and families we focus on deeds and property transfers, Florida homestead and title questions, and reviewing purchase or sale contracts and for-sale-by-owner documents. We do not currently provide closing, escrow, or settlement services — our role is the legal and planning side of your Miami-Dade County property, handled by phone and video.

Can you prepare a deed for my Miami property?

Yes. We prepare and record warranty deeds, quitclaim deeds, and enhanced life estate ("Lady Bird") deeds for Miami-Dade County property. The right deed depends on your goal — transferring to family, funding a trust, avoiding probate, or protecting Florida homestead under Art. X, §4 of the Florida Constitution. Documentary stamp tax and recording fees apply.

Can you help clear title to a Miami property after a death?

Often, yes. When an owner passes away, title to Miami-Dade County real estate usually has to be cleared before it can be sold or transferred — frequently through a petition to determine homestead status or a probate transfer. We help Miami families establish clean title and move the property to the right people.

Can you review my Miami purchase or sale contract?

Yes. We review purchase and sale contracts and for-sale-by-owner paperwork for Miami buyers and sellers, explain your obligations and risks, and suggest changes before you sign. This is advisory and document work — we do not conduct the closing or hold escrow.

How does real estate connect to my estate plan in Miami?

Your Miami home is usually your most valuable asset, so how it is titled drives whether it passes smoothly or lands in probate. We coordinate deeds, homestead, and beneficiary tools with your overall estate plan — for example, a Lady Bird deed or a funded revocable living trust can keep the property out of Miami-Dade County probate entirely.
