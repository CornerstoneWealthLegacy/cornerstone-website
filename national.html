Estate Kit

Estate planning, built for your state.

A will, living trust, and powers of attorney made for your state's law — online in about 20 minutes, with the option for a licensed attorney in your state to review your plan.

Estate Kit is self-help document software — not a law firm — and is currently available in Florida, with more states launching soon. In other states you can join the waitlist. Using the self-guided service does not create an attorney-client relationship.
