Why It Matters

The three mistakes that cost Florida landlords real money

The deposit deadlines

15 days to return, or 30 days to send the certified-mail claim notice — miss the window and you owe the whole deposit back no matter what the tenant broke. Our lease and guide put the deadlines in writing.

The defective 3-day notice

Add late fees to a 3-day notice, count the weekend, or misstate the amount, and the eviction gets dismissed — weeks lost, filing fees burned. The Notice Pack generates it correctly, every time.

Self-help "eviction"

Changing locks or cutting power is illegal in Florida (§ 83.67) and costs the landlord up to 3 months' rent per violation plus the tenant's attorney fees. The notice-and-court path is the only path — so run it right.

FAQ

Florida leases — common questions

Do I need witnesses or a notary on a Florida lease?

No — Florida eliminated the lease witness requirement in 2020. Signatures alone (including e-signatures) do it. The compliance risk isn't the signing; it's the content: deposit disclosure, radon, lead paint, and terms Chapter 83 will actually enforce.

Should my rental be in an LLC before I lease it?

Usually, yes — the entity is the landlord, which keeps tenant claims away from your personal assets. The LLC Kit forms it and the Deed Shop moves the property in; this lease then names the LLC as landlord with you signing for it.

What's included in the attorney tier?

Can I use the Notice Pack for an eviction?
