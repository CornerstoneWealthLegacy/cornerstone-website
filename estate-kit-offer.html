Common questions

Is an online will or trust legal in Florida?

Yes. Florida recognizes electronic wills (F.S. §§732.521–732.525), and trusts, powers of attorney, and health care directives can all be prepared online — as long as they meet Florida's execution formalities, which we guide you through.

How is this different from LegalZoom?

Florida Estate Kit is built specifically for Florida law, with an option for a Florida attorney (Arthur Simpson, Esq.) to personally review your plan — something national form sites don't offer.
