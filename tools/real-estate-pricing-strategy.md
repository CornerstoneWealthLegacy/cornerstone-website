# Real Estate Documents — Pricing Strategy (Florida)

Recommended pricing for the Cornerstone real estate document suite, DIY (self-serve,
attorney-prepared templates) vs. Attorney-Guided (Arthur drafts/reviews). Grounded in
market data below. Arthur sets final prices (FL Bar Rule 4-1.5: fees must be reasonable;
flat fees for document preparation are permitted).

## Market anchors (researched 2026)
- **FL attorney to DRAFT a commercial lease:** avg ~**$700** flat (bids avg ~$970). Source: ContractsCounsel.
- **FL attorney to REVIEW a commercial lease:** avg ~**$610** flat.
- **Residential lease (attorney):** commonly **$300–$700**.
- **FL real estate closing attorney (flat):** typically **$500–$1,500**.
- **DIY services:** Rocket Lawyer **$39.99/mo** unlimited templates; LegalZoom **per-doc ~$49–$89**
  (will $89, POA $49). These are generic, NOT attorney-prepared or Florida-specific, and
  do NOT offer custom addenda (the UPL gap).

## Positioning
Cornerstone sits **between** generic DIY and hiring an attorney per deal:
- **Cheaper + faster** than the $600–$970 bespoke attorney route.
- **Premium over** generic DIY ($40–$90), justified because it's **attorney-prepared,
  Florida-specific, and UPL-clean** for agents. That's the whole value.

## Recommended pricing

### Addendums (the wedge — price for impulse + volume)
| | DIY (self-serve) | Attorney-Guided |
|---|---|---|
| Per addendum | **$59–$99** | **$249–$499** (negotiated/complex) |
- Rationale: an agent can't get this elsewhere fast; well under an attorney's per-deal fee,
  high enough to signal "real legal document." Impulse-easy at $59–$99.

### Residential lease
| DIY | Attorney-Guided |
|---|---|
| **$99–$149** | **$299–$399** |
- Beats the $300–$700 attorney draft on the DIY tier; attorney-guided still competitive.

### Commercial lease
| DIY | Attorney-Guided |
|---|---|
| **$249–$399** | **$750–$1,500** |
- DIY well under the ~$700 market draft; attorney-guided priced for the negotiated/NNN deals.

### Commercial sale
| Document | Price |
|---|---|
| LOI (DIY) | **$99–$199** |
| Purchase & Sale Agreement | **Attorney-guided, $750–$2,500+** (or hourly) |
- Commercial sales are high-dollar/fact-specific → attorney-guided primary; LOI can be DIY.

## Broker / brokerage model (the recurring-revenue play)
- **Agent subscription:** **$99–$149/mo** per agent — unlimited *standard* addendums +
  discounted leases. (Mirrors Rocket Lawyer's $40/mo but Florida-specific + attorney-prepared,
  so 2–3× the price is defensible.)
- **Brokerage plan:** volume/seat pricing for offices; per-broker referral codes for
  attribution (ties to the dynamic-checkout plan).
- **À la carte** for non-subscribers at the per-document prices above.

## Launch pricing tactics
- **Anchor against the attorney bill** in the UI: "Attorneys charge ~$700 to draft this.
  Yours, attorney-prepared, for $X."
- **Intro/founder pricing** for the first brokers to seed the channel + testimonials.
- **Bundle** the lease + its exhibits/notices (don't nickel-and-dime each notice).
- Keep **attorney-guided** visibly available as the upsell on every product.

## FL Bar fee notes
- Flat fees for document preparation are allowed; must be reasonable (Rule 4-1.5) and
  clearly disclosed. Avoid "nonrefundable" language; describe scope precisely (what the
  flat fee includes vs. what triggers attorney-guided).
- Where the firm forms an attorney-client relationship (attorney-guided), use an engagement
  letter; DIY self-serve should be clearly scoped as document preparation.

Sources: ContractsCounsel (FL commercial lease draft/review costs), Rocket Lawyer /
LegalZoom published pricing.
