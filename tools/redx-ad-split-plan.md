# REDX / Paid Ad Split — Realtor lane vs. Law Firm lane

**Problem:** REDX AdBuilder ads currently mix realtor (agent) and law-firm (attorney)
messaging. That has to be separated. Showing the ads is fine; blending the two roles is
the risk.

## Why separate (3 reasons)
1. **FL Bar compliance.** Any ad promoting legal/estate-planning services is *attorney
   advertising* and must follow the Bar rules (labeled, responsible attorney named, no
   guarantees, not misleading). A realtor ad follows real-estate advertising rules
   instead. One ad can't cleanly satisfy both rulebooks.
2. **Dual-role / conflict appearance.** Advertising that you'll be someone's *agent* and
   their *attorney* invites conflict-of-interest concerns and the impression you'd wear
   both hats in one transaction. Keep them visibly distinct.
3. **Performance + lead routing.** A realtor lead and an estate-planning lead want
   different things and enter different funnels. Blended ads convert worse and pollute
   both lead lists.

## The split — two completely separate campaigns

### Lane A — REALTOR (agent)
- **Identity in ad:** "Arthur Simpson, Realtor" (agent brand / brokerage as required).
- **Tool:** REDX AdBuilder, native — its home turf.
- **Rules:** Florida real estate advertising (brokerage name, license disclosures per
  your broker's policy). NOT governed by FL Bar legal-advertising rules.
- **Offer/destination:** home valuation, buyer/seller help, listings — a realtor landing
  page or REDX form. **Not** the law-firm site.
- **Leads:** go into your real estate CRM, get realtor follow-up. Do NOT drop into the
  Cornerstone legal drip (they didn't opt into legal contact).

### Lane B — LAW FIRM (attorney) — Cornerstone
- **Identity in ad:** "Cornerstone Wealth & Legacy Law · Arthur Simpson, Esq." +
  **"Attorney advertising."**
- **Tool:** geo + demographic targeting (no uploaded contact lists). Same geography is
  fine; different creative + account framing.
- **Rules (FL Bar):** labeled attorney advertising, responsible attorney named, no
  guarantees/predictions of outcome, no misleading comparisons, only the 3 practice areas
  (estate planning, real estate **law**, elder law) — no PI.
- **Offer/destination:** `/quiz`, `/florida-estate-checklist`, `/florida-estate-kit`.
- **Leads:** opt-ins flow into the Cornerstone nurture drip (clean — they chose it).

### Hard rule
No single ad offers **both** "I'll sell your house" and "I'll do your estate plan."
If a real estate *law* angle and an agent angle overlap (e.g., selling an inherited
home), pick the hat: legal questions → Lane B attorney ad; listing/sale → Lane A realtor ad.
