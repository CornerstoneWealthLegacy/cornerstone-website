# Commercial Lease — Clause Option Library (builder spec)

Master list of clauses the commercial-lease builder should offer, beyond the core 14
articles already in the draft. Each is a TOGGLE/variant the questionnaire turns on per
deal. Concepts only — Arthur drafts/verifies the actual language. Goal: cover every
variable so the builder produces a complete, deal-appropriate lease.

Legend: ✅ already in the draft · ➕ to add as option · 🏷️ deal-type gated

## A. Lease economic structure (pick one) ✅ partial
- ✅ Gross / Modified Gross / Triple-Net (NNN) — already a variable
- ➕ Absolute Net (bondable) — tenant responsible for everything incl. structure/roof
- ➕ 🏷️ Percentage Rent (RETAIL) — % of gross sales over a breakpoint, + Base Rent;
  with gross-sales definition, reporting, records/audit, exclusions
- ➕ Base Year / Expense Stop (OFFICE gross) — tenant pays increases over a base year
- ➕ Operating-expense **gross-up** (to 95–100% occupancy) for office
- ➕ Cap on **controllable** operating expenses (already partial CAM cap — formalize)

## B. Term & options ✅ partial
- ✅ Renewal option(s)
- ➕ Early termination option (with termination fee + unamortized TI/commission recapture)
- ➕ Expansion option / Right of First Offer (ROFO) on adjacent or other space
- ➕ Contraction / give-back option
- ➕ Option to Purchase / Right of First Refusal (ROFR) on the property
- ✅ Holdover

## C. Use / operations 🏷️
- ✅ Permitted use; ✅ exclusive use (retail)
- ➕ 🏷️ Radius restriction (RETAIL) — tenant won't open competing location within X miles
- ➕ 🏷️ Continuous-operation / "go-dark" rights (RETAIL)
- ➕ 🏷️ Co-tenancy (RETAIL) — rent relief/termination if anchor leaves or occupancy drops
- ➕ 🏷️ Merchant association / marketing fund contribution (RETAIL)
- ➕ Hours of operation / building access (24/7 card access)
- ➕ Signage rights — building, monument/pylon, suite; sign criteria exhibit
- ➕ Parking — reserved vs. unreserved, ratio, visitor, EV-charging, fees
- ➕ 🏷️ Loading docks / truck court / outdoor storage / floor-load limits (INDUSTRIAL)

## D. Building systems & services
- ➕ After-hours / overtime HVAC (rate per hour)
- ➕ Utilities sub-metering vs. proportionate share (formalize)
- ➕ Janitorial / trash / recycling responsibility
- ➕ Pest control; grease trap & venting (food-use retail)
- ➕ Rooftop rights — supplemental HVAC, satellite/antenna, solar
- ➕ Backup generator / emergency power rights
- ➕ Telecom / riser access

## E. Build-out & alterations ✅ partial
- ✅ TI allowance + Work Letter (Exhibit D)
- ➕ "As-is" delivery alternative
- ➕ Trade-fixtures definition & removal rights
- ➕ Restoration / removal obligations at surrender (specify what must be removed)
- ✅ Construction-lien protection (§ 713.10)

## F. Transfer & control ✅ partial
- ✅ Assignment & subletting with consent + recapture + profit-share
- ➕ Permitted transfers WITHOUT consent (affiliates, merger, sale of entity)
- ➕ Change-of-control treated as assignment (closely-held tenant)

## G. Risk, compliance & environmental
- ✅ Insurance, waiver of subrogation, indemnity
- ➕ Business-interruption / rental-value insurance; plate-glass
- ➕ Hazardous materials — tenant covenants, compliance, environmental indemnity, Phase I
- ➕ Compliance-with-laws **cost allocation** — who pays code upgrades; ADA to common
  areas (landlord) vs. tenant's specific use/build-out (tenant)
- ➕ Sustainability / "green lease" provisions (optional)
- ➕ OFAC / anti-terrorism & anti-money-laundering representations

## H. Lender / title protection ✅ partial
- ✅ Subordination + SNDA; ✅ Estoppel certificate
- ➕ Mortgagee cure rights / notice to lender of landlord default
- ➕ No-recording covenant OR optional **Memorandum of Lease** (recordable short form)

## I. Default, remedies & dispute resolution ✅ partial
- ✅ Events of default + cure periods; ✅ landlord remedies (Ch. 83 Part I); ✅ attorney's fees
- ➕ Landlord's right to cure tenant default and bill as Additional Rent
- ➕ Tenant self-help / offset rights (heavily negotiated; default OFF, tenant-favorable)
- ➕ Security: Letter of Credit as alternative to cash deposit; burn-down schedule
- ➕ Waiver of jury trial; ➕ optional mediation/arbitration; ✅ FL venue
- ➕ Late fee ✅ + NSF/returned-payment fee

## J. Tenant protections (for tenant-side or balanced deals)
- ➕ Quiet enjoyment covenant
- ➕ Caps on operating-expense increases (see A)
- ➕ Relocation clause — and, if included, **tenant protections** (landlord pays moving,
  comparable space, size/term parity, cap on disruption) 🏷️ landlord-favorable by default
- ➕ Casualty/condemnation rent abatement specifics
- ➕ Compliance representation re: Premises delivered code-compliant

## K. Boilerplate to confirm ✅ mostly
- ✅ Notices (add email notice option), ✅ governing law/venue, ✅ entire agreement,
  ✅ brokerage, ✅ force majeure (add rent-not-excused carve-out + pandemic/deferral option),
  ✅ severability/counterparts/e-sign
- ➕ Time is of the essence; ➕ survival; ➕ authority representations; ➕ confidentiality;
  ➕ no-partnership; ➕ interpretation/headings

## Builder UX implication
Questionnaire branches by **property type** first (Office / Retail / Industrial / Mixed),
which gates the 🏷️ clauses, then asks the economic structure, then walks the option toggles
above. Attorney-guided upsell defaults ON for: percentage rent, co-tenancy, options to
purchase, environmental, self-help, and any heavily-negotiated term.
