# Meta Campaign — Florida Estate Kit
Assets: `Truestead-FLKit-Ad15` (9:16, :15) · `Truestead-FLKit-Ad40` (9:16, :42)
Landing: https://truesteadlaw.com/florida-estate-kit (UTM: `?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=flkit`)

## Campaign structure (one campaign, two ad sets)
**Objective:** Sales (website conversions). Conversion event: Purchase (Stripe) — until volume builds, optimize to a mid-funnel event (Start Kit / Lead) and upgrade later.
**Why not Traffic:** clicks are cheap and worthless; conversion optimization finds buyers.

### Ad set A — Prospecting (90% of budget)
- **Geo:** Florida (statewide). Exclude under 25.
- **Age:** 28–65+. Sweet spot is 30–55 homeowners/parents.
- **Targeting:** start **Advantage+ audience (broad)** with these as *suggestions*, not hard limits: Homeowners, Parents, Real estate, Estate planning, Life insurance. Broad + a strong creative beats narrow interest-stacking in 2026 Meta.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements, but **the :15 will auto-dominate Reels/Stories and the :40 the Feed** — upload both into one ad set and let Meta allocate.

### Ad set B — Retargeting (10% of budget)
- Website visitors 30 days + video viewers (50%+) + FB/IG engagers.
- Lead with the :40 (they already know you; give them the full pitch). Add a simple still-image ad with the og-card + price as a second creative.

## Budget & expectations
- **Start: $25–30/day** ($750–900/mo). Below ~$20/day the algorithm starves.
- First week = learning phase; judge nothing before ~50 conversions or 2 weeks.
- KPIs: CPM ($12–25 FL legal is normal), CTR >1%, cost per Start <$15, cost per Purchase target <$120 (one $399 DIY sale per ~3 days at $30/day breaks even before LTV; attorney-guided upsells and PI/estate cross-sell are gravy).
