## The pitch (one line)
**Estate planning built for your state — online DIY plus a licensed-attorney review option — starting in Florida and expanding nationally on a state-aware engine.** The trust and personalization LegalZoom lacks, at online prices.

## The problem
- ~**2 in 3 American adults** have no estate plan.
- The two options both fail people: **expensive attorneys** (slow, $1.5k–$3k+) or **generic national form sites** (one-size-fits-all, not built for your state, no real attorney — the #1 LegalZoom complaint).
- Estate documents are **state-specific** (homestead, witnessing, trust funding, community property). Generic forms quietly fail in probate.

## The solution
A state-specific online estate-planning product:
- **DIY mode** — build a will, living trust, POA, and health directives **for your state's law** in ~20 minutes.
- **Attorney-Guided mode** — a **licensed attorney in your state** reviews your plan.
- **Safety valve** — complex situations are routed to an attorney, not self-served (better outcomes, fewer invalid plans).

## Why now / why us
- AI + automation make a genuinely **state-aware document engine** feasible — our moat (built, with a code-enforced "no unverified state ships" gate).
- Founder is a **licensed Florida attorney** (Arthur Simpson, Esq.) — credibility + the FL beachhead is live and built.
- A full consumer growth stack already operating in FL: ~**800 SEO pages, 49 articles, paid-ads tracking (Meta + Google), social automation, conversion funnel.**
