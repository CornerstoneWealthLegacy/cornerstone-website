# A. ESTATE PLANNING

### EP-1 — "Will vs. Trust" (the most-searched question) — ~35s
**Hook (0–2s, bold on screen):** "A will does NOT avoid probate. Here's what does."
**Script:**
"Everyone thinks a will keeps their family out of court. In Florida, it doesn't — a will is your ticket *into* probate. What actually keeps your home and accounts out of court is a funded revocable living trust. The trust holds the assets, names who gets what, and your family skips the months and cost of probate. If you own a home in Florida, this one decision matters more than almost anything else in your plan."

### EP-2 — "The unfunded trust trap" — ~30s
**Hook:** "You paid for a trust… and it's worthless. Here's why."
**Script:**
"Here's the mistake I see most: someone sets up a living trust, signs it, files it away — and never re-titles their house or accounts into it. An unfunded trust avoids nothing. The trust only protects what's actually *inside* it. So if you have a trust, ask one question this week: is my home titled in the name of the trust? If you don't know, that's the answer to check."
**CTA:** "Not sure if your trust is funded? Grab the free Florida Estate Checklist — link below."
**Destination:** /florida-estate-checklist
**Caption hook:** Paid for a living trust? It might be doing nothing. The one box people forget to check 👇

"Welcome to Florida — now update your estate plan. Florida has its own rules for homestead, for how documents are signed and witnessed, and for who can serve as your personal representative. A will or trust drafted in New York, New Jersey, or Ohio can run into trouble here. It doesn't mean starting over — it means a Florida review so your plan actually works where you live now."

### EP-5 — "What probate actually costs" (myth-bust, data) — ~30s
**Hook:** "Probate in Florida isn't free — here's the real number."
**Script:**
"People assume probate is a quick formality. In Florida, formal administration commonly runs many months, and attorney's fees are set by statute as a percentage of the estate — often thousands of dollars, all of it public record. The frustrating part? For most families, it's avoidable with planning done ahead of time. The cheapest probate is the one your family never has to open."

# C. ELDER LAW

### EL-1 — "Power of attorney before you need it" — ~30s
**Hook:** "The day you need a power of attorney is the day it's too late to sign one."
**Script:**
"A durable power of attorney lets someone you trust step in and handle your finances if you can't. Here's the catch: you can only sign one while you still have capacity. Once a medical event hits, the option is gone — and your family is left going to court for guardianship, which is slow, expensive, and public. Signing one now is an hour that saves your family a year."
**CTA:** "Make sure yours is in place — free checklist below."
**Destination:** /florida-estate-checklist
**Caption hook:** You can't sign a power of attorney after you need it. Why families wait too long 👇

"The number that shocks families: long-term nursing care in Florida often costs well over a hundred thousand dollars a year, and standard health insurance and Medicare don't cover most of it. Without planning, a lifetime of savings can be spent down fast. Elder law planning — done early, the right way — looks at how to protect some of what you've built while still getting care. The earlier you start, the more options you have."
**CTA:** "Start the conversation — book a consult below."
**Destination:** /book
**Caption hook:** $100k+ a year for Florida nursing care — and Medicare won't cover most of it. Plan early 👇
