# Darcocean Law — Faceless Video Catalog (DRAFT)

Faceless formats to brand Darcocean at **volume** — so you can post daily without filming daily.
Pairs with the on-camera/avatar scripts (darcocean-avatar-scripts.md). Strategy: **on-camera = trust,
faceless = volume.** Use faceless to stay constant in the feed; drop your face/clone in regularly so
people bond with a human. All formats FL Bar compliant (no guarantees/outcomes, no "best/leader/win,"
attorney advertising, responsible attorney named, AI disclosed where used). Contingent on name lock.

Tools you already have: **Higgsfield** (B-roll/visuals), **ElevenLabs** (VO / your voice clone),
**HeyGen** (avatar — for the on-camera-feel ones), **Opus Clip** (repurpose long→short), **Postiz**
(schedule/post). Captions burned in. Navy/gold grade. End-card every time.

Standard end-card: logo + "Darcocean Law · Arthur Simpson, Esq. · FL Bar #529265 · Attorney advertising ·
General information, not legal advice." If AI voice/visuals used: add "Contains AI-generated content."

================================================================
## HOW TO READ THIS — 3 jobs, ~14 formats
================================================================
Every video should do ONE job. Don't mix.
- **BRAND** — make Darcocean feel premium, trustworthy, recognizable (the look, the thesis, the name).
- **EDUCATE** — teach FL law facts → builds authority, gets shares/saves, feeds AI-SEO.
- **CONVERT** — push the DIY builder, the quiz, or a consult.

Recommended weekly mix (5 posts): 2 EDUCATE, 1 BRAND, 1 CONVERT, 1 on-camera/clone.

================================================================
## A. BRAND FORMATS (atmosphere + recognition)
================================================================

### F-1 — Brand Atmosphere Film (no words, or one line) — BRAND
**What:** 8–15s. Cinematic ocean-depth / Florida coast / quiet home interior B-roll, navy/gold grade,
logo resolve, tagline "Depth Meets Distinction." No talking. Pure vibe + recognition.
**Recipe:** Higgsfield (slow ocean depth, light through water, calm coastline) → logo animation → tagline.
ElevenLabs ambient or licensed calm music. **Use:** pinned reel, ad bumper, story filler, channel trailer.
**Why faceless works:** builds the *look* of the brand so the navy/gold becomes instantly "you."

### F-2 — Tagline / Quote Card Motion — BRAND
**What:** 6–10s. A single brand line animates on navy (kinetic type). "Most people meet the law in
pieces. We bring it together." / "Calm on the surface. Strong underneath." Logo close.
**Recipe:** Text animation (CapCut/Canva/Remotion template) on navy bg + subtle wave motion + gold accent.
**Why:** infinitely repeatable, 30 in an afternoon, trains the brand voice.

### F-3 — "One Roof" Visual Thesis — BRAND
**What:** 15–20s. Three icons (house / scroll / handshake) drift apart, then snap together under the
Darcocean mark. VO or text: the integration thesis (estate + property + elder, coordinated).
**Recipe:** Higgsfield or simple motion graphics; ElevenLabs VO (your clone). **Why:** this is your
*differentiator* — show it, don't just say it.

================================================================
## B. EDUCATE FORMATS (authority + shares + AI-SEO fuel)
================================================================

### F-4 — "Did You Know — Florida Edition" Fact Drops — EDUCATE
**What:** 15–25s. One surprising, true FL law fact. e.g. "In Florida, if you die without a will, the
state's intestacy law decides who gets your home — not you." Text-on-screen + B-roll + VO.
**Recipe:** Higgsfield B-roll (FL home, courthouse, document) + ElevenLabs VO + bold caption. **Cite the
statute on-screen** (e.g., Fla. Stat. §732.101) — citations boost AI-SEO + credibility.
**Why:** highly shareable, positions you as the FL authority, endless supply of facts.

### F-5 — Myth vs Fact — EDUCATE
**What:** 15–25s. "MYTH: A will avoids probate." [beat] "FACT: In Florida, a will *goes through* probate.
A funded revocable trust is what keeps you out." Split navy/gold visual, two-beat reveal.
**Recipe:** text animation + VO. **Why:** myth-busting is the single most-shared legal format; corrects a
real misconception → trust. (Keep it general info, never advice to an individual.)

### F-6 — "3 Documents Every Florida ___ Needs" Listicle — EDUCATE
**What:** 20–35s. Numbered reveal. "3 documents every Florida homeowner needs before closing." /
"4 things your Florida estate plan is missing." Each item = one card + B-roll.
**Recipe:** Remotion or Canva template (swap text per topic = scale), ElevenLabs VO. **Why:** listicles get
saved; saves = reach. Each one seeds a blog post (AI-SEO) and vice versa.

### F-7 — Statute / Number Reveal — EDUCATE
**What:** 10–15s. A single number lands big. "$0 — what your family pays in probate if your trust is
funded correctly." or "30 days — how long a Florida landlord has to return a deposit (§83.49)."
**Recipe:** kinetic number on navy, gold accent, one-line context. **Why:** punchy, scroll-stopping,
citation-anchored. Great for both estate AND your real-estate doc line.

### F-8 — "What Happens If..." Scenario (faceless storytelling) — EDUCATE
**What:** 25–40s. A calm, narrated scenario. "A Florida father passes with no will. He owns a home, a
boat, and a bank account. Here's what the state decides..." Walk through it, end on the lesson.
**Recipe:** Higgsfield narrative B-roll (home, family items, document) + ElevenLabs VO. **Why:** stories
out-perform facts; emotional but tasteful. Strong for the estate/elder audience.

### F-9 — FL Law Update / Newsjack — EDUCATE
**What:** 20–30s. "Florida just changed ___. Here's what it means for homeowners." When a relevant
statute or rule changes. Text + B-roll + VO.
**Recipe:** fast turnaround template. **Why:** timeliness = algorithm boost + AI-SEO freshness signal +
positions you as current. (Verify the change before posting — accuracy is the gate.)

================================================================
## C. CONVERT FORMATS (DIY builder + quiz + consult)
================================================================

### F-10 — DIY Builder Screen-Walkthrough — CONVERT  ★ your secret weapon
**What:** 20–40s. Screen recording of the actual DIY document builder — show how fast/easy it is to
generate a FL will or lease addendum. "Watch me build a Florida will in under 5 minutes." Speed-ramp it.
**Recipe:** screen-record start.html/the builder → speed up → ElevenLabs VO + captions → CTA. **Why:**
this is your *product demo* for the younger DIY buyer — shows the thing works, removes intimidation,
drives the actual purchase. No face needed. **Make a library of these — one per document type.**

### F-11 — Problem → Product Bridge — CONVERT
**What:** 20–30s. Open on a real pain ("Lawyers quoted you $2,500 for a will?"), bridge to the DIY option
("Florida-specific, attorney-built, do it yourself for a fraction — or upgrade to attorney-guided").
**Recipe:** B-roll + text + VO + CTA to the builder. **Why:** frames your pricing wedge; converts the
price-sensitive younger client. Keep claims accurate (no "cheapest/best" — say what it *is*).

### F-12 — Quiz Hook — CONVERT
**What:** 10–15s. "Does your Florida estate plan have a gap? Take the free 3-minute quiz." One question
teased on screen. **Recipe:** quick text + B-roll + CTA. **Why:** low-friction top-of-funnel capture.

## PRODUCTION SYSTEM (how to actually make a lot of these)
================================================================
1. **Templatize** F-2, F-4, F-5, F-6, F-7 in Canva or **Remotion** (data-driven = swap text, render 20).
2. **Script in batches** — write 20 fact drops / myths at once; I can generate these for you.
3. **B-roll library** — build a Higgsfield bank (ocean depth, FL homes, documents, coastline, courthouse,
   family moments) you reuse across dozens of videos. Generate once, use forever.
4. **Voice** — record yourself OR ElevenLabs clone; one voice session covers many videos.
5. **Long → short** — film one 10-min on-camera talk, run **Opus Clip** → 8–12 faceless-style shorts.
6. **Schedule** — load a month into **Postiz**; consistency beats virality.
7. **Repurpose loop** — every EDUCATE video ↔ a blog FAQ (AI-SEO). Same content, two channels.
