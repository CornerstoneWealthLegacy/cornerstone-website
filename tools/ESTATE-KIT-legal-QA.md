## How to use this
1. Open **`ESTATE-KIT-statute-register.csv`** (Numbers/Excel) — one row per unique citation
   (238 total: 218 Florida statutes + 20 federal/tax regs), with the documents each appears in.
2. For each row: open the **live statute** at flsenate.gov, confirm the document's use is
   correct, mark **Live statute confirmed = Y**, add **initials + date**.
3. Sign the block at the bottom when the full pass is complete.
4. **Re-run every July** (Florida statutes amend July 1) — see protocol below.

> ⚠️ **Auto-classification is a starting point.** The CSV guesses FL-vs-federal by chapter
> number; a few federal regs may be mislabeled (e.g. **27 CFR §479** ATF/NFA rules in the
> Gun Trust auto-tagged as "FL"). Confirm type as you verify — that's part of the check.

## Pre-verified core formalities (the valid-vs-void linchpins) — checked this pass
Verified against current (2024–2025) Florida Statutes. These are the ones that, if wrong,
*void* a document — and they are correct:

| **§732.503** | Will, Codicil | Self-proving affidavit: testator acknowledges + witness affidavits before notary; RON allowed ✓ |
