### REL-2 — "Quitclaim vs. warranty deed" — ~30s
**Hook:** "Signing a quitclaim deed? Know what you're giving up."
**Script:**
"A warranty deed guarantees the seller actually owns the property and can transfer clear
title — you're protected if a problem surfaces later. A quitclaim deed makes no such
promise; it transfers only whatever interest the person happens to have, which could be
none. Quitclaims have their place — between family, into a trust — but using the wrong
deed can cost you the protection you assumed you had."

### REL-3 — "The lady bird deed" — ~30s
**Hook:** "Florida has a deed that skips probate on your home — and keeps you in control."
**Script:**
"It's an enhanced life estate deed — a 'lady bird' deed. You keep full control of your
home while you're alive: sell it, mortgage it, change your mind, anytime. At death it
passes to whoever you name, with no probate on the house. It isn't right for every
situation, but for many Florida homeowners it's a simple, powerful tool worth asking a
