# Addendum Builder — Strategy & Catalog (the agent UPL wedge)

The single sharpest product in the real estate line: an attorney-built **addendum generator**
that produces the custom addenda agents legally CANNOT draft themselves.

## The legal mechanics (why this works)
- FL real estate licensees may **fill in blanks** on approved standard forms (FR/BAR, FAR/BAR)
  but **drafting custom provisions/addenda is the practice of law** → an agent who drafts one
  commits **UPL**.
- **Cornerstone is a law firm.** A licensed FL attorney preparing legal documents is the LAWFUL
  practice of law — not UPL. The tool is the firm's **attorney-prepared** drafting instrument.
- So: the agent routes the deal to our system; the system produces an addendum **"Prepared by
  Cornerstone Wealth & Legacy Law, PLLC."** The agent never drafts. We serve demand agents can't.
- **The pitch =** the legal reason: *"You can't draft it. We can, in minutes, the right way."*

## Copyright guardrail (non-negotiable)
- **Never reproduce the FR/BAR / FAR/BAR contract** (copyrighted by Florida Realtors + The
  Florida Bar). The addendum **references and attaches** to it — it does not copy it.
- Standard incorporation language: *"This Addendum is attached to and made part of that certain
  [FR/BAR AS IS Residential Contract / FAR-BAR Contract] dated ___ between [Seller] and [Buyer]
  for the property at ___ (the 'Contract'). In the event of conflict, this Addendum controls.
  All other terms of the Contract remain in full effect."*

## UPL-clean structure
- Firm named as **preparer** on every addendum (attorney-prepared).
- Attorney-verified templates (standing rule). Attorney-guided upsell for anything non-standard.
- Ideal customer relationship: the **principal** (buyer/seller) is the firm's client, agent
  facilitates — OR the agent purchases attorney-prepared documents for their deal. Either way the
  **firm is the legal author**, not the agent.
- Clear disclaimers; this is attorney advertising; the firm represents the party it prepares for.
- Keep distinct from Arthur's own agent activity (dual-role line).

## Ethical boundary (do NOT cross)
- **Serve genuine need; never manufacture it.** Do not design the product to push agents into
  doing (or needing to avoid) UPL, or to engineer artificial complexity so a deal "becomes" legal
  work we then sell. That risks FL Bar Rule **4-5.5** (a lawyer shall not assist UPL) and is an
  unfair-practice problem.
- The demand is already large and organic: real deals constantly exceed the standard form
  (escalation, appraisal gap, contingencies, leaseback, 1031, LLC vesting, assignment). We capture
  the **real moment of need**, fast and compliantly — that is the entire value.
- Stickiness comes from being the obvious fast/cheap/compliant option + honest education on when
  an addendum is genuinely required — NOT from inflating complexity.

## Builder UX
1. Agent/principal selects which base contract (FR/BAR AS-IS, FAR/BAR, commercial) — by NAME only.
2. Enters the deal facts (parties, property, contract date, the specific need).
3. Picks the addendum(s) needed from the catalog (multi-select).
4. System generates attorney-prepared addendum(s), referencing the base contract, firm as preparer.
5. Output: clean PDF, ready to attach to the agent's filled-in standard form.

## Business model
- **Per-addendum** flat fee (fast, cheap vs. hourly attorney) — impulse-easy for agents.
- **Broker/agent accounts** + volume pricing + per-broker referral codes (repeat B2B).
- **Attorney-guided** upsell for negotiated/complex addenda (financing, 1031, commercial).
- Speed is the product: minutes vs. days. Position as the agent's "addendum desk."
