for Darcocean Law (FL Bar compliant), and a bold INVESTOR/umbrella voice for Darcocean the

## 1) DARCOCEAN LAW — Client-facing brand story
(Warm, depth, "trusted family advisor." No dominate/win/leader/ultimate/guarantee language.)
================================================================
**Tagline:** Depth Meets Distinction.
**One-liner:** Florida real estate, estate, and elder law — coordinated under one roof.

Most people meet the law in pieces: one lawyer for the closing, another for the will, a third
when a parent needs care — and none of them ever talk to each other. Darcocean Law was built to
close that gap. We bring real estate law, wills, trusts, estates, and elder law together so your
plan actually fits together.

Founded by **Arthur Simpson, Esq.** — a Florida attorney and licensed real estate broker with
20+ years of experience — Darcocean Law pairs hands-on transactional know-how with thoughtful,
proactive planning. Arthur has spent his career closing complex deals *and* protecting families:
drafting estate plans, guiding probate, resolving title and contract issues, and helping older
clients plan for long-term care and protect what they've built.

**The name.** *Darc* is the quiet strength and hidden depth behind good counsel — and the forward
arc of planning ahead. *Ocean* is the enduring nature of protection done right: calm on the
surface, powerful underneath. We believe real security isn't transactional. It runs deep, and it
lasts for generations.

**What we do**
- **Real Estate Law** — contract review, due diligence, title resolution, closings, and dispute
  resolution, with attorney-led oversight that helps transactions close cleanly.
- **Wills, Estates & Trusts** — customized plans to protect and transfer wealth: revocable and
  irrevocable trusts, estate-tax-aware strategies, and legacy planning built around your values.
- **Elder Law** — compassionate guidance on long-term care planning, Medicaid asset protection,
  powers of attorney, guardianship, and veterans' benefits — preserving dignity and hard-earned
  assets.

**Why one roof matters.** Because Darcocean coordinates law and real estate together, your estate
plan supports your property, and your transactions are backed by proactive legal strategy. Fewer
gaps. Less back-and-forth. One team that sees the whole picture.

**Our promise of approach (not outcome):** deeper analysis, deeper protection, deeper relationships.

*Welcome to Darcocean Law — where legacies are secured.*

## 3) BRAND VOICE GUIDE
================================================================
**Personality:** the trusted, seasoned advisor — calm, confident, precise, warm. Premium without
being flashy. "Strength in depth," not "look how big we are."

**Say (client/law):** protect, coordinate, plan ahead, depth, clarity, peace of mind, your legacy,
your family, attorney-led, proactive, integrated, generations.

**Avoid — especially in anything client-facing (FL Bar Rule 4-7):**
- Superlatives/leadership claims: "best," "#1," "leading," "global leader," "top," "ultimate"
- Conqueror language: "dominate," "unstoppable," "shield and sword," "rewrite the rules," "crush"
- Outcome promises/implications: "win," "guaranteed," "we'll get you X"
- Unsubstantiated comparatives ("better than other firms")
- Anything that could mislead about results, specialization, or relationships

**Always (client/law):** "attorney advertising," name Arthur Simpson, Esq. as responsible attorney,
"general information, not legal advice." No guarantees. Only the practice areas offered.

**Visual identity (per the logo review):** flat, scalable "D + wave/eye" mark — NOT the 3D chrome
look. Color system by vertical: **Darcocean Law = deep navy + gold/silver** (trust, premium);
Darcocean Real Estate = teal/blue. Locked wordmark "DARCOCEAN" doubles as the spelling fix.
Need: vector + one-color + reversed versions, stacked + horizontal lockups, D-only mark for
avatars/favicon.

**Taglines in play:** "Depth Meets Distinction." (primary) · "Where Legacies Are Secured." ·
"Strength in Depth. Vision Beyond." (umbrella).
